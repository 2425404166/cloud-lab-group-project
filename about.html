About CyberWise

Safer technology begins with informed people.

We turn cybersecurity from a technical mystery into practical knowledge that anyone can use.

Our purpose

Cyber awareness should feel clear, useful, and human.

Every day, people make decisions that affect their digital safety: opening an email, choosing a password, joining a network, or sharing information. CyberWise exists to make those moments safer.

Our goal is to help individuals, families, students, and teams understand common threats without fear or unnecessary jargon. Better awareness creates stronger habits, and stronger habits create a safer internet for everyone.

Group project

Building awareness through practical digital safety education

CyberWise is a group project created to educate users about common cybersecurity threats and the everyday actions they can take to stay safe online. The website presents clear guidance on topics such as strong passwords, phishing, device updates, social engineering, and secure internet use.

Our team combined research, content planning, web development, and repository management to create an accessible resource that encourages individuals and communities to make informed decisions in the digital world.

The CyberWise approach

Pause. Check. Protect.

Use this simple three-step mindset whenever something online asks for your attention, information, or trust.

  1. 01

    Pause

    Slow down when a message feels urgent, surprising, or too good to be true.

  2. 02

    Check

    Verify the sender, link, request, and context through a trusted channel.

  3. 03

    Protect

    Report suspicious activity and keep your accounts, devices, and data secure.
